    You get where someone was. Pick where they're going. If you pick their real future, you see their whole arc. If you don't, you've grafted two people together, and the new person joins the collective "we" at the bottom.

    Each square is one writer. The pattern shows what they wrote: rows are their three "we"s and their "I", columns are past, present and future, and each stroke is a sentence.
